Happy Birthday Mrs Lucas!!

For my show & tell, the two form tutors will be competing against each other in battleship. To play battleship, each player attacks their opponent's board in hopes to sink a ship. The player who sinks all the ships of their opponent first wins. Have fun & good luck (the whole form is watching)!!

To ensure you can't see the other person's ships, the boards temporarily disappear after a move is made and the next player needs to press R on the keyboard to carry out their turn. To change ears, press E on the keyboard when it's your turn.
